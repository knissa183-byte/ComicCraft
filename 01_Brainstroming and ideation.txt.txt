COMICCRAFT – BRAINSTORMING AND IDEATION

1. Project Idea
   ComicCraft is an AI-assisted comic creation project that helps users transform a simple story idea into a comic-style story with scenes and dialogues.

2. Problem Statement
   Creating a comic manually can take a lot of time and requires writing and creative planning skills. Users may find it difficult to develop a complete comic story from a simple idea.

3. Proposed Solution
   ComicCraft provides a simple way to organize a story idea into comic-style scenes and dialogues using AI assistance.

4. Target Users
   • Students
   • Beginners interested in comic creation
   • Story writers
   • Creative content creators

5. Key Idea
   User Story Idea → AI Assistance → Scenes + Dialogues → Comic Panels

6. Main Features
   • Story idea input
   • Character details
   • Genre selection
   • Scene-based comic creation
   • Dialogue generation
   • Comic panel output

7. Expected Outcome
   The project aims to provide an easy and beginner-friendly approach for creating structured comic content from a simple story idea.
